# FoodieGo – Food Delivery App UI/UX Design

## 1. Project Overview
FoodieGo is a modern and user-friendly food delivery app UI/UX design created using Figma. The application allows users to explore restaurants, browse food menus, add food items to their cart, and place orders conveniently.The design focuses on a simple interface, attractive food images, easy navigation, and a smooth ordering experience.

 # Objectives
- To design an attractive and user-friendly food delivery application.
- To help users discover restaurants and food items easily.
- To provide food categories, menu details, and prices.
- To simplify the cart and checkout process.
- To create a smooth food ordering and delivery tracking flow.
- To improve user experience through modern UI/UX principles.

  # Design Highlights
- **Modern UI:** Clean layouts with attractive food images.
- **Color Palette:** Orange, red, white, and light grey.
- **Typography:** Clear and readable fonts.
- **Food Cards:** Food images, names, prices, and ratings.
- **Reusable Components:** Buttons, search bars, food cards, and navigation bars.
- **Interactive Prototype:** Connected screens to demonstrate the ordering journey.
- **Mobile-First Design:** Layout designed for mobile screens.

  # Features
- **Home Screen:** Displays popular restaurants, recommended dishes, and food categories.
- **Search Screen:** Helps users search for restaurants and food items.
- **Restaurant Screen:** Shows restaurant details, ratings, and menus.
- **Food Details Screen:** Displays food images, descriptions, and prices.
- **Cart Screen:** Allows users to review selected items and quantities.
- **Checkout Screen:** Displays delivery address, order summary, and payment options.
- **Order Tracking Screen:** Shows the stages of an order.
- **Order Confirmation:** Displays a successful order message.
- **Easy Navigation:** Quick access to Home, Search, Orders, and Profile.
